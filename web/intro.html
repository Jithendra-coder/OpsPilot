A BETTER WAY THROUGH AN INCIDENT

From first signal
to verified recovery.

Each step leaves a record. The investigator gathers evidence and explains its reasoning; people retain authority over change.

01

Bring the signal together

Start with an incident or controlled replay. Keep logs, metrics, traces and changes in the incident’s scope.

⌁
02

Test the possible causes

Compare competing explanations against observed evidence. See what supports a cause—and what remains uncertain.

⌕
03

Put a human at the action boundary

Recommendations use typed, allowlisted actions. A different approver reviews the exact proposal before an operator can run it.

⌑
04

Verify, then close the loop

Server-owned checks decide whether symptoms cleared. Preserve the approval, execution and result in the incident record.

✓

BUILT AROUND A CLEAR BOUNDARY

An AI recommendation
is never permission.

Investigation is read-only. Remediation stays in an isolated local simulator, behind a separate approver, with recovery determined by server-side checks.
